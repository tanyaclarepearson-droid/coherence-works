One connected model

Each lens answers a different question. Together they help describe what adults can shape, how conditions shift, what may become visible, and when learning may be easier to carry forward.

01 · SHAPE3F · ConditionsForm, Frequency, and Fidelity describe the fit, recurrence, and coherence adults can tend.
02 · SHIFTTemporal dynamicsConditions emerge, stabilize, fade, remain latent, and return as readiness and context change.
03 · EMERGECREAConditions may support Resonance, Emergence, and Agency. This names a generative pathway, not a guaranteed sequence.
04 · NOTICE5R · SignaturesDevelopmental signals help make change perceptible while it is still taking shape.
05 · DEEPENDPZ · DurabilityA threshold range where coherent patterns may become more stable, transferable, and self-sustaining.

Conditions have life cycles. The path can circle back with new meaning.

  1. EmergenceA new condition enters the field.
  2. Peak impactNovelty and participation may rise.
  3. StabilizationThe practice becomes more familiar.
  4. Fade / backgroundIts immediate effect may be less visible.
  5. Dormant potentialThe condition remains available in memory and culture.
  6. ReactivationIt returns as readiness or context shifts.
  7. IntegrationIt can shape future practice and new conditions.

Re-entry is part of the model: renewed conditions can open another cycle of resonance, emergence, and agency.
